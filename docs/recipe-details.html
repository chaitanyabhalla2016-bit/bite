Creamy Garlic Pasta

Rich creamy pasta loaded with garlic, herbs, parmesan cheese, and a silky smooth sauce perfect for cozy cravings.

4.8 Rating
20 mins
540 kcal

Ingredients

  • 200g Pasta
  • 4 Garlic Cloves
  • 1 Cup Heavy Cream
  • Parmesan Cheese
  • Fresh Parsley
  • Olive Oil

Cooking Steps

1

Boil The Pasta

Bring salted water to a boil and cook the pasta until perfectly al dente. Drain and set aside.

2

Prepare The Sauce

Sauté garlic in olive oil, then add heavy cream, parmesan cheese, and herbs for a silky smooth sauce.

3

Mix Everything

Toss the cooked pasta into the sauce and coat evenly until creamy and flavorful.

4

Serve Fresh

Garnish with parsley and parmesan cheese. Serve hot and enjoy immediately.
